About

Taylor Powers builds brands in public: on race cars, jerseys, fight nights and broadcast.

As VP of Brand at SuperFile, Taylor took the company to market and built its sports and entertainment program, with Trackhouse Racing in NASCAR and MotoGP and a global partnership with UFC and WWE, managing more than $20 million in production.

Before that, as Managing Director and Partner at Realm Agency, Taylor ran operations and production for FLEX, EGO and SKIL, and activated FLEX’s front-of-jersey sponsorship with LAFC. It all started in feature-film visual effects at Wylie Co. and Digital Domain.

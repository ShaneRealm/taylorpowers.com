About

Brand, creative development and operations, from the first idea through final delivery.

Taylor Powers is a brand and creative production executive. As VP of Brand at SuperFile, Taylor took the company to market, built and led a 25+ person department across brand, strategy, creative, partnerships and production, and ran partnerships with Trackhouse Racing in NASCAR and MotoGP, UFC, WWE and LAFC, managing $20M+ in production budgets.

Before that, as Managing Director and Partner at Realm Agency, Taylor directed operations, business development and production for EGO, SKIL and FLEX, overseeing 20,000+ assets and 200+ product launches. It started in Hollywood production and visual effects for Marvel Studios, ABC, Fox, Netflix, Legendary and Lionsgate.

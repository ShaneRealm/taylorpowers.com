About

As VP of Brand at SuperFile, Taylor took the company to market and built its sports and entertainment program, with Trackhouse Racing in NASCAR and MotoGP and a global partnership with UFC and WWE, managing more than $20 million in production.

Before that, as Managing Director and Partner at Realm Agency, Taylor ran operations and production for clients including FLEX, EGO and SKIL, and activated FLEX’s front-of-jersey sponsorship with LAFC.

Taylor began in feature-film visual effects at Wylie Co. and in Digital Domain’s Digital Human Group. That discipline of schedules, pipelines and final delivery still runs every project.
